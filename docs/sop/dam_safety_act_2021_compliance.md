# Statutory Dam Safety Act 2021 Compliance Report

## 1. Regulatory Context
Under Section 28 & 31 of the **Dam Safety Act 2021 (Act No. 41 of 2021)** passed by the Parliament of India:
- Every specified dam owner must prepare a comprehensive Emergency Action Plan (EAP).
- The EAP must establish clear dam-break inundation maps, evacuation time estimates, and early warning disseminations.

## 2. JalRakshak Compliance Matrix
- **Section 29 (Inundation Mapping)**: Verified against 2D flexible mesh unsteady flow hydrodynamic modeling.
- **Section 30 (Early Warning System)**: Deterministic Evacuation Window Engine (EWE) integrated with siren and tactical radio triggers.
- **Section 31 (Annual Mock Drills)**: Pre-rendered Full HD simulation player enabling realistic operator training and review.
