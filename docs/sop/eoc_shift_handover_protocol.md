# Emergency Operations Center (EOC) Shift Handover Protocol

## 1. Shift Transition Handover Requirements
Outgoing and Incoming Incident Commanders must jointly complete this physical and digital logbook review:

### Handover Checklist:
- [ ] **Hydraulic Baseline**: Review active scenario (Central, Conservative, Upstream Overtopping).
- [ ] **Sensor Telemetry**: Confirm zero dropout on Koteshwar, Malidewal, and Tipri gauge feeds.
- [ ] **Evacuation Route Status**: Verify Route R02 transit clearance and limiting segment status.
- [ ] **Shelter Readiness**: Confirm Bageshwar high-ground camp logistics and food/water provisions.
- [ ] **Radio Check**: Confirm line-of-sight VHF repeater link with Surkanda Devi summit.
