# Acoustic Warning Siren Protocol for Valley Communities

## 1. Siren Tone Specifications
- **Alert (Warning - T+00 to T+15)**: Continuous 440 Hz tone for 3 minutes.
- **Evacuate (Imminent Danger - T+15 onwards)**: Rising and falling wail (400 Hz to 800 Hz) in 4-second cycles for 5 continuous minutes.
- **All Clear**: Steady dual-tone (520 Hz + 660 Hz) for 2 minutes.

## 2. Acoustic Coverage Radius
- Hillside ambient attenuation: $-6\text{ dB}$ per doubling of distance.
- High-power electronic siren (130 dB @ 30m): Reliable acoustic range is $3.2\text{ km}$ along valley corridor.
