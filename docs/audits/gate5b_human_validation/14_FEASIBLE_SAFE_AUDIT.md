# 14 — Feasible vs Safe Audit

**Project:** JalRakshak Emergency Decision-Support System  
**Audit Purpose:** Enforce Non-Absolute Safety Language  
**Status:** PASS  

---

## 1. Language Rules

- **Allowed:** `FEASIBLE`, `LOW MARGIN`, `INFEASIBLE`, `DATA GAP`.
- **Prohibited:** `SAFE`, `GUARANTEED SAFE`, `SAFE ROUTE`, `RISK-FREE`.

---

## 2. Operational Notice
Every view displays the persistent operational notice:
> *"Feasible under current scenario and configured assumptions. Not a guarantee of physical safety."*
